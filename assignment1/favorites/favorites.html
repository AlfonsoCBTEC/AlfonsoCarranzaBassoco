Favorites

  • ⭐ Blinding Lights

    I chose Blinding Lights because it has an extremely catchy melody and an energetic 1980s-inspired sound. It is also one of The Weeknd's most recognizable songs.

  • ⭐ The Hills

    I chose The Hills because it represents the darker side of The Weeknd's music. The production, vocals, and lyrics create a mysterious and intense atmosphere.

  • ⭐ Save Your Tears

    I chose Save Your Tears because it combines an emotional story with an upbeat synth-pop sound. I also like how the song connects with the visual style of the After Hours era.
