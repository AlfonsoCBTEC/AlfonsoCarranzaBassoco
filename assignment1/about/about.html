Short biography

Abel Makkonen Tesfaye, known professionally as The Weeknd, was born on February 16, 1990, in Toronto, Canada. He began releasing music anonymously online in 2010 and gained attention for his dark and atmospheric R&B style. His popularity grew after releasing mixtapes such as House of Balloons, Thursday, and Echoes of Silence.

He later became one of the biggest artists in the world, combining R&B, pop, electronic music, and cinematic visuals. His albums have achieved worldwide success, and he is known for creating a distinctive artistic identity throughout his career.

Career highlights

  • Released his first mixtape, House of Balloons, in 2011.
  • Gained international recognition with Beauty Behind the Madness.
  • Released the highly successful album Starboy in 2016.
  • After Hours became one of his most successful albums.
  • His song "Blinding Lights" became one of the most successful songs in music history.
  • Released Dawn FM in 2022, continuing his cinematic and conceptual style.
  • Has won multiple Grammy Awards and other major music awards.
